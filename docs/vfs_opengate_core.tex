\note{This identity shows how the growth of the shared will--action coordinate is weighted symmetrically by the other pole. The formula is an identity; the existential law is that will and action become strongest when neither pole is isolated.}

\section{Paschal Triad of Filtrum Lucis}
\subsection*{Why the higher derivatives are needed}
The derivative ladder is not only computational. It reveals the inner Paschal structure of the light-filter: the Cross is detected by the third derivative, while the entrance into death and the exit from death are detected by the two symmetric zeros of the fourth derivative.

\subsection*{The Cross: zero of the third derivative}
\[
\boxed{\Phi'''(x)=k^2T_F(1-T_F)(1-2T_F).}
\]
Since \(T_F(0)=1/2\),
\[
\boxed{\Phi'''(0)=0.}
\]
\note{The point \(x=0\) is the Filtrum Cross: synergy and resistance meet, and the sign of the cathartic term inverts.}

\subsection*{The two gates of Anastasis: zeros of the fourth derivative}
\[
\boxed{\Phi^{(4)}(x)=k^3T_F(1-T_F)(1-6T_F+6T_F^2).}
\]
The quadratic factor has two roots at the transmissivity levels
\[
\boxed{s_\pm=\frac12\pm\frac{1}{2\sqrt3}.}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\boxed{x_\pm=\pm\frac1k\ln(2+\sqrt3).}
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{\Phi^{(4)}(x_-)=0,\qquad \Phi'''(0)=0,\qquad \Phi^{(4)}(x_+)=0,}
\]
and the exact symmetry is
\[
\boxed{x_-+x_+=0.}
\]
\note{The Cross is the geometric midpoint between the entrance into death and the exit from death. This is a differential icon of the Paschal passage, not a claim that time itself is symmetric.}

\subsection*{Paschal triad}
\[
\boxed{\begin{array}{ccl}
\Phi^{(4)}(x_-)=0 &:& \text{Entrance into death / Great Friday},\\[2pt]
\Phi'''(0)=0 &:& \text{Cross / Katharsis / Great Saturday},\\[2pt]
\Phi^{(4)}(x_+)=0 &:& \text{Exit from death / Anastasis / Resurrection}.
\end{array}}
\]
\[
\boxed{x< x_-:\ \Phi^{(4)}>0,\qquad
x_-<x<x_+:\ \Phi^{(4)}<0,\qquad
x>x_+:\ \Phi^{(4)}>0.}
\]
\note{Between \(x_-\) and \(x_+\) lies the symbolic death interval of Filtrum Lucis; outside it the fourth-order sign returns to life.}

\subsection*{Fourth derivative support for \(u\)}
In Pleroma Christi, \(\sigma=0\), so \(x=u\). To compute the fourth derivative of the Filtrum part one needs \(u^{(4)}\). From
\[
\boxed{u^2=VF+\varepsilon}
\]
one obtains
\[
\boxed{u^{(4)}=\frac{(VF)^{(4)}}{2u}-\frac{4u'u'''+3(u'')^2}{u}.}
\]
By Leibniz,
\[
\boxed{(VF)^{(4)}=V^{(4)}F+4V'''F'+6V''F''+4V'F'''+VF^{(4)}.}
\]
\note{These binomial coefficients explain why the fourth derivative is the first place where the full symmetry of the Paschal passage becomes visible.}

\subsection*{Pleroma Christi reduction}
In the Pleroma Christi regime, \(\sigma=0\), hence all derivatives of \(\sigma\) vanish and \(x=u\). The fourth-order expression becomes
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{An}_{\rm Pl}(t)=&\frac{d^4\Syn}{dt^4}
+\Phi^{(4)}(u)(u')^4
+6\Phi'''(u)(u')^2u''
+3\Phi''(u)(u'')^2\\
&+4\Phi''(u)u'u'''
+\Phi'(u)u^{(4)}.
\end{aligned}}
\]
In deep Epektasis, the higher Filtrum derivatives fade:
\[
\boxed{\lim_{t\to\infty}K_{\rm Pl}(t)=0,
\qquad
\lim_{t\to\infty}\mathrm{An}_{\rm Pl}(t)=0.}
\]
\note{Katharsis and Anastasis complete as events; Epektasis continues as motion into fullness.}

\section{Sophianic Folding Feedback in the Core}
\subsection*{Status of the refinement}
The original Open-Gate core remains the baseline system. Sophianic Folding is added here as a \emph{conservative refinement}: it does not remove the Open-Gate balance, but separates Sophia into an available component and an embodied morphological component.

The guiding chain becomes
\[
\boxed{\Igate\longrightarrow\lambda\longrightarrow q_{\rm fold}\longrightarrow\lambda_{\rm form}\longrightarrow P.}
\]
\note{Sophia does not only accumulate. Once the old Vessel-form becomes insufficient, part of Sophia can be embodied as form.}

\subsection*{Available Sophia and embodied Sophia}
In the unrefined core, \(\lambda\) denotes Sophia as a single state variable. In the folding-refined core, \(\lambda\) is read as \emph{available Sophia}, while \(\lambda_{\rm form}\) records the portion embodied into form:
\[
\boxed{\lambda_{\rm tot}=\lambda+\lambda_{\rm form}.}
\]
The original model is recovered by setting
\[
\boxed{q_{\rm fold}=0,\qquad Q_{\rm fold}=0,
\qquad \lambda_{\rm form}=0.}
\]

\subsection*{Pre-fold Sophia production}
Before morphological feedback is applied, the Open-Gate Sophia production is
\[
\boxed{\dot\lambda^{(0)}=-\dot\sigma+\Igate(t)
=(\delta u-\gamma)\tanh(\kappa\sigma)+\Igate(t).}
\]
This is exactly the original Open-Gate law.

\subsection*{Folding activation index}
The folding mode is governed by a threshold index
\[
\boxed{A_{\rm fold}=c_0\lambda+c_1\dot\lambda^{(0)}-a_0.}
\]
The stabilizing coefficient is
\[
\boxed{B_{\rm fold}=b+2c_1\eta_{\rm fold}\lambda,
\qquad B_{\rm fold}>0.}
\]
The folding amplitude follows the normal-form dynamics
\[
\boxed{\dot q_{\rm fold}=A_{\rm fold}q_{\rm fold}-B_{\rm fold}q_{\rm fold}^{3}.}
\]
The corresponding folding intensity is
\[
\boxed{Q_{\rm fold}=q_{\rm fold}^{2}.}
\]
On the stable folded branch,
\[
\boxed{Q_{\rm fold}^{\ast}=\frac{A_{\rm fold}}{B_{\rm fold}}
\quad\text{when }A_{\rm fold}>0.}
\]
If \(A_{\rm fold}<0\), the old form \(q_{\rm fold}=0\) remains stable.

\subsection*{Folding-regulated Sophia flow}
Folding acts as a negative feedback on available Sophia:
\[
\boxed{\dot\lambda
=\dot\lambda^{(0)}-\eta_{\rm fold}Q_{\rm fold}\lambda.}
\]
The embodied component receives exactly this transferred part:
\[
\boxed{\dot\lambda_{\rm form}=\eta_{\rm fold}Q_{\rm fold}\lambda.}
\]
Therefore the total Open-Gate balance is preserved:
\[
\boxed{\frac{d}{dt}\bigl(\sigma+\lambda+\lambda_{\rm form}\bigr)=\Igate(t).}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\boxed{\sigma(t)+\lambda(t)+\lambda_{\rm form}(t)=C_0+\Grec(t).}
\]
\note{The apparent loss term in \(\dot\lambda\) is not destruction of Sophia. It is embodiment of Sophia into the Vessel-form.}

\subsection*{Effective Sophia damping}
On a quasi-stationary folded branch, \(Q_{\rm fold}\) increases the effective damping of available Sophia:
\[
\boxed{\gamma_{\rm eff}=\gamma_{\lambda}+\eta_{\rm fold}Q_{\rm fold}.}
\]
Here \(\gamma_{\lambda}\) denotes any phenomenological damping or integration rate used in reduced Sophia-flow models. In the exact Open-Gate core above, the same role is played by the feedback term \(-\eta_{\rm fold}Q_{\rm fold}\lambda\).

Thus,
\[
\boxed{Q_{\rm fold}>0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\dot\lambda<\dot\lambda^{(0)}.}
\]
\note{Folding is the morphological branch of Open-Gate stabilization: it turns excess available Sophia into stable form.}

\subsection*{Core meaning}
The refined core chain is
\[
\boxed{V,F\longrightarrow u\longrightarrow\sigma\longrightarrow\lambda
\longrightarrow q_{\rm fold}\longrightarrow\lambda_{\rm form}\longrightarrow P.}
\]
In words:
\[
\boxed{\text{Will becomes action; resistance becomes Sophia; Sophia becomes form; form releases being.}}
\]
