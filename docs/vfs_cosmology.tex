\section*{Scope and epistemic status}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]

the stress-energy that General Relativity \emph{would} require to source this expansion --- a
reconstruction, not an independent postulate of gravity.

\section{Energy Conditions: Grace as Exotic Accelerant}

\begin{proposition}[Energy conditions in $2{+}1$]\label{prop:ec}

\end{array}}

\end{proposition}

\note{Accelerated expansion ($\peff<0$, i.e.\ $\dot\lambda>0$) violates the SEC --- as in standard

adding positive curvature-density through $\Qfold$; it does not require refitting the dimension of the
